Added extended string tunings, a dedicated tuning editor, and beta support for Wear OS.

Features Added:
- Tunings with up to 12 strings.
- Presets for Guitar, Bass & Ukulele with extra strings.
- New Tuning Editor screen allowing you to rename, add/remove strings & directly set the note/octave of strings.

Beta Release of Choona for Wear OS:
- Tune right from your wrist with the new watch app for Wear OS devices.
- Tailored UI for small watch screens.
- Full feature parity with the mobile app.
